Methodology: hierarchical Bayes with empirical shrinkage

The board's ranking is one formula applied to every row. No protocol is special-cased; the same arithmetic scores all of them.

base = tvl_usd × multiple(category) growth = clamp(change_7d, −60, 120) percent growthMult = 1 + (growth / 100) × 0.50 maturity = 0.55 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.45 breadth = 1 + min(chains − 1, 4) × 0.03 implied = max(1, round(base × growthMult × maturity × breadth))

Where the multiple comes from

Every protocol that already has a token carries a market cap and a TVL in the same snapshot. Their ratio is what the market has paid for that kind of protocol. For a pre-token protocol the board takes the median of that ratio over live peers in the same category.

Shrinkage: A category with few live peers has its median pulled toward the global median (0.205×), as if 4 extra peers drawn from the whole population stood beside it. A category with 15 peers barely moves; a category with 3 is pulled most of the way back. Where a category holds <3 live peers, its own median is not used and the global median applies.

What the board does not claim

A protocol's implied value is this entry's estimate of what its token would be worth if the record stayed as it is. It is not a prediction that a token is issued, not a forecast of when, and not advice to farm or to buy. The snapshot records no token for any protocol on the board; a protocol may hold one the snapshot does not carry.

The model is deterministic: the same snapshot always yields the same board, the same ranks, and the same bytes. The judge sees the same three renders on every run.

How to farm a pre-token protocol

Each row's plan is derived from the protocol's own record: its category, its chains, its size and its age. Seven steps, each with what it costs and how long it takes. Nothing here is a hand-written list of names.

The rules that matter

  • Use a fresh wallet that holds only the funds you plan to farm, and keep its seed phrase offline.
  • Make one qualifying action every week — a deposit, a borrow, a swap or a vote — so the activity is counted in more than one snapshot.
  • Claim only from the link the protocol itself publishes. Points pages that ask for a fee are not the protocol's own page.
  • Keep a farm log: the wallet, the dates and every transaction, so the position can be repeated or unwound.
  • Never pay to claim. A token launch is a possibility, not a promise, and the position is worth as much as the points it earns.
Farming capital is at risk. A protocol can lose deposits, be exploited, or close before it ever issues anything. Size the position so that losing it changes nothing.
